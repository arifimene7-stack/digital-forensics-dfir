# Scope and Authority

## Authorized Environment
This project uses only systems, files, and datasets that the analyst owns or has explicit permission to examine.

## Scope
- Evidence acquisition
- Evidence integrity
- Filesystem and disk analysis
- Memory and host artifacts
- Timeline reconstruction
- Incident investigation

## Out of Scope
- Unauthorized access
- Examination of third-party private data
- Modification of original evidence

## Authorization
Training evidence must have a documented source and permission for analysis.

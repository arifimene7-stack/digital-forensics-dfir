# Acquisition Decision Matrix

| Investigation Need | Logical Acquisition | Physical Acquisition | Live Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Collect selected files | Suitable | Suitable | Sometimes suitable |
| Examine unallocated space | Not normally sufficient | Suitable | Not suitable |
| Collect running processes | Not suitable | Not suitable | Suitable |
| Collect active network information | Not suitable | Not suitable | Suitable |
| Preserve a broad storage-level view | Limited | Suitable | Not suitable |
| Minimize collection scope | Suitable | Less suitable | Depends on scope |

## Decision Factors

- Investigation objectives.
- Evidence volatility.
- Device availability.
- Encryption status.
- Legal authorization.
- Time and storage constraints.
- Potential impact on the original system.

## Conclusion

No single acquisition method is appropriate for every case.
The investigator must select the method according to the
evidence required and the investigation scope.
